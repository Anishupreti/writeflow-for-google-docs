Privacy policy (draft)

WriteFlow for Google Docs™ Effective date: 10/11/2026. Contact: helloanishupreti@gmail.com. Provided by: Anish Upreti.

The short version

  • WriteFlow works only inside the Google Doc you open it in. It cannot open your other files.
  • It reads your text to show counts, chapters, search and insights inside the sidebar. It never changes your document.
  • Your text is not sent to us, to advertisers, or to any AI service. Analysis happens in your browser.
  • What it saves (goals, writing history, pins) is stored in your own Google account, visible only to you, not to collaborators.
  • No ads, no selling data, no tracking across sites.

What Google lets WriteFlow do

When you install it, Google asks you to allow two permissions:

Permission (as Google words it) What it is used for
View and manage documents that this application has been installed in Reading the text of the document you opened it in, and moving your cursor when you click a chapter, search result or pin. Google offers no read-only version of this permission; WriteFlow never edits, adds or deletes your text.
Display and run third-party web content in prompts and sidebars inside Google applications Showing the sidebar and its windows (wide table, Night Reader, Help).

The Writer's Desk is a web page served by the same Apps Script project, running as you. It only reads what the sidebar saved in your Google account (above); it cannot open your documents. Its link opens your document in Google Docs like any other link.

It does not ask for access to your Google Drive, Gmail, contacts or any other document.

What is read, and where it goes

Your document's text is read by Google's Apps Script service (part of Google's infrastructure, running as you) and passed to the sidebar in your browser. All analysis (word counts, chapters, search, Story Bible mentions, insights, structure) runs there. The text is not sent to WriteFlow's developers or any third party.

What is saved, and where

In your Google account (Apps Script "user properties": private to you, not shared with other people who edit the document): - Settings: theme, search options, sprint length, recently used commands, your name if you type one for snippets or reports. - Per document: the document's title, so it can be labelled in Settings → Storage and backup. - Per document: goals and deadline; words written per day and active writing time (up to about six weeks); sprint history; sections you chose to skip from the word count; story-beat placements. - Per document, small pieces of your text used to find places again: for each pin and for "where you stopped", the first 40 characters of that paragraph and about 40 characters around the spot, plus the pin's name. - For the Writer's Desk (your private start page): for up to four documents you used recently, the document's title, link and chapter name, today's word count, goal and writing streak, and up to 500 characters of the lines you last wrote. You can turn off the lines in Settings, hide a document from the Desk, or remove it with Forget this document.

In your browser (the sidebar's storage, on this device only): - A copy of the document's text, so the sidebar opens instantly and the Lifeboat works offline. - Project data you create in WriteFlow (for example notes, questions and outline items as those features arrive), in the browser's IndexedDB database. It stays on this device and isn't synced; use Storage and backup → Download backup to keep a copy or move it to another computer. - The Lifeboat scratchpad, if you write in it. - An unfinished sprint's timer and Night Reader preferences.

AI review with your own key (optional, off by default). If AI review is offered in your version and you switch it on in Settings, the API key you enter is stored only in this browser, for the sidebar. It is never saved to your Google account, included in backups or error reports, or sent to us. Requests go directly from your browser to the provider you chose (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google), under that provider's terms and privacy policy. The connection test sends only the words "Reply with OK." and no text from your document. Anything else that would be sent is shown to you first in the Context Builder.

Plans and payments (when paid plans are switched on). - In your Google account: the date your free trial started, and, if you activate Pro, your license key and its last checked status (active, ended), with the time it was checked. Nothing else about the purchase is stored by WriteFlow. - License checks: when you activate a key, and about once a week afterwards, the sidebar sends your license key and our product ID directly to Gumroad's license service, to confirm the subscription is active. No document text is sent. If Gumroad can't be reached, WriteFlow keeps working on the last confirmed status for a grace period. - Gumroad is the seller of the subscription. It collects your payment details, email and billing country under its own privacy policy; WriteFlow never sees your card details. As the creator, we can see in Gumroad the purchase details it shows sellers (such as your email, country and what you bought), which we use only to provide support and handle refunds.

Project backups you download contain your goals, history, pins, stage and project data for one document, plus the small pieces of text listed above. They never contain your manuscript. Restoring one adds missing items and never deletes anything.

Files you download (backups, snippet images, Writing Journey reports, Obsidian exports, CSV tables) are created in your browser and saved only where you save them.

Error reports

If something goes wrong, the sidebar sends the developers a short error report so problems can be fixed. It contains: the app version, which part of the sidebar was open, the error message and code location with anything in quotation marks removed, your browser name and version, and the document's size as a range (for example "50–100k words"). It never contains your document's text, title, or your name. Reports are kept in Google Cloud Logging for [30] days. You can switch error reports off at any time in Settings → Error reports. They are on by default during the beta and off by default from public launch.

Diagnostics (Settings → Diagnostics) shows sizes and timings only and is sent to no one unless you copy it into an email yourself.

What we never do

  • Sell, rent or share your data.
  • Use your text to train AI, or send it to any AI service.
  • Show ads or use your data for advertising.
  • Let humans read your text. WriteFlow's developers have no access to it.

Google API Services User Data Policy. WriteFlow's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

  • One document: Settings → Forget this document deletes everything saved for it in your Google account, its project data in this browser, and the sidebar's copy. Your document is not touched.
  • Other documents: Storage and backup lists every document WriteFlow has saved data for, with a Forget button for each.
  • Everything: remove the add-on (Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons → WriteFlow → Remove), then revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Clear site data for docs.google.com in your browser to remove local copies.
  • Ask us: email [support email]. We hold no copy of your text or saved data, so there is nothing for us to delete beyond error reports, which expire on their own.

Children

WriteFlow is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their information.
