Billing questions

When does the trial start, and do I need a card?

It starts the first time you open WriteFlow, and lasts 30 days. No card or account is needed.

What happens when the trial ends?

You move to the Free plan. Nothing is deleted: your notes, outline, history and backups stay, and Pro tools are paused until you subscribe.

How do I activate Pro after buying?

Your receipt email contains a license key. In WriteFlow, open Settings → Plan, paste the key and press Activate.

Can I use it on more than one Google account?

Yes, one license works on up to 3 Google accounts that you use.

How do I cancel?

From your Gumroad library, any time. Pro stays on until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Within 14 days of a payment, email support@YOUR-DOMAIN.example for a full refund, no questions asked.

Who handles payment and tax?

Gumroad sells the subscription as the merchant of record: it handles payment, sales tax and VAT, and sends your receipts. WriteFlow never sees your card details.

What if I’m offline when my license needs checking?

Pro keeps working. WriteFlow checks your license about once a week and allows two weeks without a connection before asking you to go online.
